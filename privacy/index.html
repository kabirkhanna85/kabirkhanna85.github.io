Analytics

This personal academic website uses Google Analytics automatically when you visit. It does not display a consent banner or visitor analytics controls.

Analytics measures pages visited, visit sources, browser and device information, approximate location, and interactions such as outbound links and file downloads. Analytics uses cookies to distinguish visits. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled.

Analytics cookies are configured to expire six months after their most recent update.

Google processes the Analytics data. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Hosting and contact

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information when serving its pages; see GitHub's Privacy Statement. Links to papers and other external websites are covered by those sites' own privacy policies.
