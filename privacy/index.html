Privacy

Optional analytics

This personal academic website uses Google Analytics only if you choose Accept analytics. You can browse the website and download the CV without accepting.

With your permission, Analytics measures pages visited, visit sources, browser and device information, approximate location, and interactions such as outbound links and file downloads. Analytics uses cookies to distinguish visits. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled.

No Analytics tag loads and no Analytics data is sent to Google before you accept. Your choice is remembered in this browser for six months. Analytics cookies are configured to expire six months after their most recent update.

You can change your choice at any time using Analytics preferences at the bottom of each page. Declining stops future Analytics collection in this browser and removes this site's Analytics cookies. It does not erase data already collected by Google.

Google processes the Analytics data. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Hosting and contact

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information when serving its pages; see GitHub's Privacy Statement. Links to papers and other external websites are covered by those sites' own privacy policies.
