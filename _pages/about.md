Hi! I’m Kabir, a physics PhD student at the [University of Geneva](https://www.unige.ch/) working with [Prof. Romain Vasseur](https://sites.google.com/view/romain-vasseur/home). At leading order, I am interested in all things quantum-condensed-matter. Thus far, my PhD has revolved around applying tools from field theory and statistical mechanics to understand universal measurement-related phenomena in many-body systems both in and out of equilibrium. Have a look at my papers below to learn more. 

Before my PhD, I studied [Engineering Design](https://ed.iitm.ac.in/) with an integrated master’s in [Quantum Science & Technology](https://quantum.iitm.ac.in/programmes/) at the [Indian Institute of Technology Madras](https://www.iitm.ac.in/). I then spent a year at the [University of Oxford](https://www.ox.ac.uk/), where I obtained an [MSc in Mathematical and Theoretical Physics](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/courses/msc-mathematical-and-theoretical-physics).
